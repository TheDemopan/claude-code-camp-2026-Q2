# tbaMUD World Map & Guide

## Main Locations

### Midgaard - Central Hub
- **Temple Square**: Central area connecting to Temple, Clerics' Guild, Grunting Boar Inn
  - North: Temple of Midgaard
  - West: Clerics' Guild
  - East: Grunting Boar Inn
  - South: Market Square

- **Market Square**: Central marketplace
  - North: Temple Square
  - South: Common Square
  - East: Main Street (crossing)
  - West: Main Street

- **Common Square**: South of market square
  - North: Market Square
  - East: Dark Alley
  - West: Poor Alley
  - South: The Dump (entrance)

- **Main Street**: Runs east-west through town
  - Crossing point: Market Square area
  - North: General Store, Weapon Shop
  - South: Guild of Swordsmen
  - East: Leads out of town

### Guilds & Trainers

#### Guild of Swordsmen (Swordmasters)
- **Location**: South of Main Street (eastern section)
- **Route**: Market Square → E → E → S
- **Layout**:
  - Entrance Hall: Guarded by knight, ATM available
  - Bar of Swordsmen: North exit to Main Street, South to Practice Yard
  - Tournament and Practice Yard: Guildmaster here, well leads down (DO NOT GO DOWN)
    - **Items**: Dagger, Candles, Torch
- **Guildmaster**: Sharpens axes, gives practice sessions for skills

#### Guild of Thieves
- **Location**: South of Dark Alley
- **Guarded**: Access restricted beyond entrance hall

#### Clerics' Guild
- **Location**: West of Temple Square

### Inns & Taverns

- **Grunting Boar Inn**: East of Temple Square
- **Grubby Inn**: South of Poor Alley (contains: beggar, Filthy bartender)

### Other Locations

- **The Bakery**: 
  - Route: Grunting Boar Inn (w) → Temple Square (s) → Market Square (w) → Main Street (n)
  - NPCs: Baker (sells baked goods)
  - Shop List:
    - A danish pastry (7 gold)
    - A bread (14 gold)
    - A waybread (71 gold)

- **Pet Shop**: South of Main Street (crossing point)
- **General Store**: North of Main Street (crossing point)
- **Weapon Shop**: North of Main Street (eastern section)

- **The Dump**: South of Common Square
  - Contains garbage and sewer entrance
  - Exit to sewers via ladder up from below

### Sewers (Dangerous - Avoid)
- Interconnected system beneath city
- Multiple junctions and dead ends
- **Exit**: The Quadruple Junction Under The Dump → climb up ladder
- **Monsters**: Sewer rats, spiders

### Newbie Zone (Recommended Level 1+)
- **Route**: From Temple → North → Behind Temple Altar → North → Great Field → East → Entrance to Newbie Zone
- **Areas**:
  - The Beginning Of The Passage: Entry point, creepy crawlers
  - The Dirty Hallway: Pet dragon, passages
  - A Nexus: Intersection, some doors closed
  - More Of The Hallway: Continues hallway
  - Another Corner: Newbie monsters, east door closed
  - A Brighter Hallway: Creepy crawlers
  - The End Of The Passage: Multiple newbie monsters, exit to open air
  - An Open Field By The Great Field: Exit from passage

### The Great Chessboard of Midgaard (Advanced - Above Recommended Level)
- **Route**: From Great Field → West on dirt path → West through rusty gate
- **Description**: "This zone is above your recommended level" warning displays
- **Areas**:
  - The Great Chessboard Of Midgaard: Archway with rusty gate
  - White/Black Squares: Chess board squares with pawn NPCs
  - **NOTE**: Massive Minotaur likely located in this advanced zone
- **Monsters**: Chess pawns (location holders), likely boss-tier creatures

## Game Mechanics

### Combat Commands
- **score**: Shows character stats (HP, MP, MV, AC, level, experience)
- **consider [target]**: Compares your level to a target; rough difficulty estimate (doesn't account for HP/special attacks)
- **kill [target]** or **hit [target]**: Starts combat with a target
- **flee**: Escapes combat if suitable exit exists (may lose experience)
- **wield [weapon]**: Equips a weapon from inventory
- **wear [armor]**: Equips armor/clothing
- **inventory**: Shows what you're carrying

### Practice System
- Practice skill command: `practice [skill name]`
- Limited practice sessions per character
- Sessions regenerate over time
- Can only practice with guildmaster in guild hall
- Use `practice` alone to see available skills and proficiency levels

### Skills Known
- **kick**: Basic martial arts skill, learned at level 1
  - Proficiency: (bad) at level 1

### Hunger & Thirst
- Character gets hungry and thirsty over time
- Can obtain food/drink from NPCs like the baker
- Status updates periodically

### Experience & Leveling
- Gain experience by defeating monsters
- Skills improve with practice and use

## Movement Commands
- `n` (north), `s` (south), `e` (east), `w` (west)
- `u` (up), `d` (down)
- `look` - examine current location
- `recall` - teleport to home location (if available)

## Important Notes
- **DO NOT** go down the well in the practice yard (leads to dangerous sewers)
- Always save character before risky actions
- Practice sessions are limited resource - use wisely
- Some areas are restricted based on guild membership or level
